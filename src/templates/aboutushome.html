Enhancing Pharmaceutical Transparency

PharmaTrace is dedicated to revolutionizing the pharmaceutical industry by ensuring transparency and safety through innovative traceability solutions. Our mission is to provide accurate and accessible information about pharmaceutical products to consumers and stakeholders.

Scanning a QR code on medicine packages reveals essential details such as the medicine's name, batch number, expiry date, and manufacturing date. This, combined with blockchain technology, ensures secure and accessible data. The goal is to create a user-friendly system for easy access to medication information, facilitating informed decision-making in healthcare. Integrating QR codes on medicine packages serves as a gateway to vital details, enhancing accessibility and data security. Blockchain technology strengthens system integrity using hashing for tamper resistance. This security measure allows users to confidently trace a tablet sheet's journey from manufacturing to distribution, empowering informed healthcare decisions and fostering transparency in the pharmaceutical supply chain.
